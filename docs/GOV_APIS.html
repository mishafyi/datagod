1. FRED — Federal Reserve Economic Data

What: 800K+ economic time series — GDP, CPI, unemployment, interest rates, housing, money supply, and more.

Auth: Free API key from fred.stlouisfed.org

curl -s "https://api.stlouisfed.org/fred/series/observations?series_id=GDP&api_key=YOUR_KEY&file_type=json"
SeriesDescription
GDPGross Domestic Product
UNRATEUnemployment Rate
CPIAUCSLConsumer Price Index
FEDFUNDSFederal Funds Rate
DGS1010-Year Treasury Rate
MORTGAGE30US30-Year Mortgage Rate
SP500S&P 500 Index

2. EDGAR — SEC Corporate Filings

What: Every public company's structured financial data (XBRL), filing history, and metadata. 19,000+ companies.

Auth: User-Agent: YourName your@email.com

# Company metadata + filings
curl -s "https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000320193.json" -H "User-Agent: Jack jack@gmail.com"

# All financial facts
curl -s "https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000320193.json" -H "User-Agent: Jack jack@gmail.com"

# Cross-company revenue comparison
curl -s "https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/frames/us-gaap/Revenues/USD/CY2023.json" -H "User-Agent: Jack jack@gmail.com"

Verified: Apple Inc., AAPL, Nasdaq, SIC 3571

3. USAspending — Federal Spending

What: Every federal dollar — contracts, grants, loans. $6T+/year.

Auth: None

# Search spending (POST)
curl -s -X POST "https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/search/spending_by_award/" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"filters":{"keywords":["artificial intelligence"]},"fields":["Recipient Name","Award Amount"],"limit":5}'

# Agency list
curl -s "https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/references/toptier_agencies/"

4. Census Bureau

What: Demographics for every geography — population, income, education, housing, business patterns.

Auth: Free key optional — signup

# Population by state
curl -s "https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs1?get=NAME,B01001_001E&for=state:*"

# Median income by state
curl -s "https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs1?get=NAME,B19013_001E&for=state:*"

Verified: Alabama 5,108,468 / Alaska 733,406 / Arizona 7,431,344

5. BLS — Bureau of Labor Statistics

What: Employment, wages, CPI, PPI. Source of monthly jobs report.

Auth: Free key optional — signup

curl -s "https://api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v2/timeseries/data/LNS14000000"

Verified: Feb 2026 unemployment = 4.4%, Jan 2026 = 4.3%

6. Treasury Fiscal Data

What: National debt, federal revenue, spending, interest rates, exchange rates.

Auth: None

# National debt (to the penny)
curl -s "https://api.fiscaldata.treasury.gov/services/api/fiscal_service/v2/accounting/od/debt_to_penny?sort=-record_date&page[size]=3"

Verified: $38.9 trillion (2026-03-13)

7. FEC — Federal Election Commission

What: Every political donation and expenditure in federal elections.

Auth: Free key (DEMO_KEY works)

curl -s "https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/candidates/?api_key=DEMO_KEY&office=P&per_page=5"

Verified: 6,744 presidential candidates returned

8. Congress.gov

What: Every bill, vote, member, committee, amendment, treaty.

Auth: Free key (DEMO_KEY works)

curl -s "https://api.congress.gov/v3/bill?api_key=DEMO_KEY&limit=5&format=json"

9. openFDA

What: 20M+ drug adverse events, recalls, device reports, food enforcement.

Auth: None

curl -s "https://api.fda.gov/drug/event.json?search=patient.drug.openfda.brand_name:aspirin&limit=3"

10. ClinicalTrials.gov

What: 500K+ clinical trials worldwide.

Auth: None

curl -s "https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.cond=cancer&pageSize=5"

11. SAM.gov

What: Federal contracting opportunities, entity registrations, exclusions.

Auth: Free key from sam.gov (~24 hours)

curl -s "https://api.sam.gov/opportunities/v2/search?api_key=YOUR_KEY&limit=5&postedFrom=01/01/2026"

12. EIA — Energy Data

What: Energy production, consumption, prices. Oil, gas, renewables, electricity.

Auth: Free key (DEMO_KEY works)

curl -s "https://api.eia.gov/v2/petroleum/pri/gnd?api_key=DEMO_KEY&frequency=weekly&data[0]=value&length=5"

Verified: 14 datasets (coal, oil, gas, electricity, renewables, etc.)

13. PatentsView

What: Patent analytics — inventors, assignees, citations, classifications.

Auth: Free key from patentsview.org

curl -s "https://search.patentsview.org/api/v1/patent/?q={...}&per_page=5" -H "X-Api-Key: YOUR_KEY"

14. OpenFEMA

What: Every disaster declaration since 1953, NFIP flood claims, assistance payments.

Auth: None

curl -s "https://www.fema.gov/api/open/v2/DisasterDeclarationsSummaries?\$top=5&\$orderby=declarationDate%20desc"

Verified: Latest: SEWER LINE COLLAPSE, PA, 2026-02-20

15. Federal Register

What: Every rule, proposed rule, executive order, and agency notice. Real-time.

Auth: None

curl -s "https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?per_page=5&order=newest"
